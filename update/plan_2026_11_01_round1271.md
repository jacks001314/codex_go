## What changed

Rust rejects uploaded audio *inside* the turn runner: the turn is accepted, the
accompanying text is recorded, and the turn then fails with
`UnsupportedOperation("Uploaded audio file inputs are not enabled in this build.")`.
Go refused the request at admission, so nothing was recorded and the client saw
a JSON-RPC error instead of a failed turn.

- `turn/start` no longer rejects uploaded audio; the turn runner detects it,
  records the filtered input (text and every other input preserved) and
  completes the turn as failed.
- The failure maps to `codexErrorInfo: badRequest` with the
  `unsupported_operation` analytics kind, matching Rust's
  `CodexErrorDetails::UnsupportedOperation`.
- `turn.TurnUserInput.MarshalJSON` emitted only `url` for audio, dropping
  `fileId`; the uploaded-file arm now survives serialization.
