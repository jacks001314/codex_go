## Findings

- #44360 removed `AgentsOverviewView::new`'s `exit_on_cancel` argument and made
  list cancellation call `state.focus_composer()`, preserving the draft and
  cursor while returning from the list to the inline task composer.
- Upstream #45255 (`516f2780fd`) later replaced that inline composer with direct
  new-session shortcuts and removed `AgentsOverviewFocus`, the view-level
  `ChatComposer`, and the composer hints. At the `cbe16d525c` baseline

  even while `connection_notice` is shown, so Escape closes the dashboard.
- Go follows the successor design: the command center has no overview composer
  (`tui/agents_overview` documents that the task composer is gone), and
  `tui/tea/agents_overview.go` closes on Escape through
  `Cancel -> CompletionCancelled -> closeAgentsOverview` without quitting or
  switching tasks.
