## Goal

Mirror #41392: add the shared Guardian context primitives that synchronous
review and asynchronous scoring will both consume, so sections are scoped,
collected in registration order, and fail as a whole when required evidence is
missing.

## Implementation

- `guardiancontext/context.go` (new package):
  - `ContextTarget` (`sync`, `async`) and `SectionScope`
    (`shared`, `sync_only`, `async_only`) with `Includes`.
  - `SectionInput` carrying the target and the parent conversation history,
    typed as `[]eventmap.ResponseItem` (the Go counterpart of Rust's
    `ResponseItem`).
  - `SectionContributor`, whose `Scope` is checked before `Contribute` runs.
  - `SectionRegistry.Register`/`Collect`: registration order is preserved,
    non-matching scopes are skipped without invocation, optional sections
    return `(nil, nil)`, and the first error aborts collection without
    returning partial context.
  - `SectionError`/`MissingRequiredEvidence` plus the
    `ErrMissingRequiredEvidence` sentinel for `errors.Is`.
- `guardiancontext/entry.go`: `ConversationTranscriptEntryKind` with the
  user/developer/assistant/protected-assistant/tool-call/tool-output/
  node-repl-output/reasoning variants and their transcript `Role`, and
  `ConversationTranscriptEntry` retaining `Text` and `OriginalBytes`.
- `guardiancontext/truncate.go`: `TruncateText`, Rust's UTF-8-safe
  prefix/suffix truncation with `<truncated omitted_approx_tokens="N" />` and
  marker-only output when the budget cannot fit the marker.
- `context/node_repl_review_evidence.go`: `GuardianTruncateText` now delegates
  to the shared primitive while keeping Rust's
  `was_truncated = len(content) > approx_bytes_for_tokens(token_cap)` result.
- `appserver/guardian_reviewer.go`: per-entry transcript caps use the shared
  primitive, matching Rust's `collect_transcript` (which calls
  `guardian_context::truncate_text`). This also replaces the previous
  `…N tokens truncated…` marker with Rust's
  `<truncated omitted_approx_tokens="N" />`.
