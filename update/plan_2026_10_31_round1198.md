## 58428

config/plugin_apps.go merges [plugins.<id>.apps] tables into the effective
apps configuration:

- Only plugins that are explicitly enabled or allowed by the _default policy
  contribute; plugin-local _default tables are dropped.
- Plugin names are applied in reverse sorted order, so the alphabetically
  earlier plugin wins conflicts; the explicit top-level apps table is applied
  last.
- The merge runs in mergeConfigLayers (config read/preview) and
  LoadWithOptions (runtime effective config), so app tool policy sees it.
- apps.* origins are re-projected from the contributing layer paths.
