## Evidence

- `setup_lock_windows.go` adds the machine-wide
  `Global\CodexSandboxSetup` mutex. The setup helper takes it only for
  account-provisioning modes; uninstall takes a 5-second timeout.
- `uninstall_cleanup_windows.go` disables both accounts with NetUserInfo level
  1008, enumerates their processes with WTS, re-checks each opened token SID,
  terminates and waits up to 5 seconds, and waits for matching LSA logon
  sessions to disappear. A preparation failure restores the original flags.
- `CleanUpPackagedWindowsSandbox` removes only `.sandbox`,
  `.sandbox-secrets` and `.sandbox-bin` under an absolute pinned home, runs
  the desktop callback while accounts stay disabled, and continues independent
  cleanup after failures. The group is retained after any earlier failure.
- `CleanUpLegacyWindowsSandbox` shares the same preparation and still keeps
  every user file.
