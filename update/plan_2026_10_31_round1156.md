## Findings

- Rust #44671 keeps voice sessions alive under load: capture blocks and pending
  frames older than `MAX_PROCESSING_DELAY` (500 ms) are dropped together with
  the APM history (`capture_worker.rs`, `processing.rs`); a rejected device
  callback sets `capture_dropped` and the worker drains the backlog instead of
  failing closed; incoming stale or saturated media is dropped silently
  (`incoming.rs`); muted capture sends paced, pre-encoded silence without
  device buffers (`audio_track.rs`); playback backlog no longer fails the sink.
- Go already had the non-fatal incoming/playback drops, paced `sendSilence`,
  and the fixed `HelperExitStage` contract with parent-side stage decoding and
  `ConnectionError` classification.
- Gap found: `audioProcessingDeadline` was declared but never read. `pumpCapture`
  forwarded arbitrarily old capture blocks and pending frames, and
  `pcmPipeline.droppedCapture` was written on queue overflow but never consumed,
  so a saturated queue resumed with stale audio and retained APM history.

## Changes

- `voicehost/audio_pipeline.go`: add `takeDroppedCapture` and `drainCapture`
  so the service loop can observe a rejected device callback and discard the
  retained backlog.
- `voicehost/media.go`: `pumpCapture` drops capture blocks older than
  `audioProcessingDeadline`, drops late pending frames before send, and
  consumes the overflow flag; each drop clears the partial frame and resets the
  APM (`dropLateCapture`), preserving queue bounds and mute generations.
- `voicehost/processing_deadline_like_rust_test.go`: covers stale capture
  recovery, late pending-frame suppression, backlog drain after queue
  saturation, and APM history reset.
