## Gap: editor kill/yank not dispatched

Rust's composer textarea (bottom_pane/textarea.rs) dispatches the full editor
keymap: kill to line end/start (with boundary-newline semantics), kill whole
line, and yank (paste the single-entry kill buffer at the cursor).

Implemented:

- `tui/tea/editor_kill_yank.go`: `applyEditorKillYankKey` (routed before the
  Vim dispatch) mirrors Rust: ctrl-k cuts cursor->line-end (or the trailing
  newline at the line end), ctrl-u cuts line-start->cursor (or the preceding
  newline at the line start), kill_whole_line cuts the whole line, ctrl-y
  pastes the single-entry kill buffer at the cursor; the buffer survives
  composer clears (submit / slash dispatch).
- `tui/tea/vim_mode.go`: `vimSetCursorAtByteOffset` - SetValue-based edits
  (kill/yank and the Vim delete/operator/paste helpers) now keep the cursor at
  the edit point instead of jumping to the last row, matching Rust.
