Upstream commit: `fb0781b9eee6d2da741b984bed9dde95834d909d`

## Result

Not applicable by construction.

The upstream hardening protects Rust's filesystem-backed synthetic-mount
registry from a writable bind exposing registry state. Go does not create that
registry. Missing or denied read targets are backed directly by anonymous
memfds through bubblewrap `--ro-bind-data` in
`sandbox/linuxsandbox/linux.go` (`appendUnreadableRootBwrapArgs`). There is no
registry temporary directory, generated symlink, or registry path that a
writable bind can expose.
