# Non-TUI audits: MCP analytics, subagent forks, attachment persistence intent

## `#45716` MCP auth and approval analytics

Go captures trusted connector auth-failure classifications before model-facing
rewrites, emits app/skill connector selection and use analytics, and reports
approval outcomes with the expected null/approval classification. MCP and
app-server analytics tests pass.

## `#51329` Remove partial-history subagent forks

Only `none` and `all` fork modes remain; the legacy partial-history aliases are
removed from the control surface and spawn validation. Agent, app-server, and
exec fork tests pass.

## `#51517` Attachment persistence intent

Attachment upload requests carry the originating thread's ephemeral intent on
every upload, and request-time image preparation marks ephemeral uploads
accordingly. Attachment-store and model image-preparation tests pass.
