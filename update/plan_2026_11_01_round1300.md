## The gap

Round 1298 made every host prepare the environment provider with Rust's
Noise-first bootstrap, but `appserver.EnvironmentManager.ApplyProviderSnapshot`
still switched only on stdio and "everything else is WebSocket". A snapshot entry
with `EnvironmentTransportNoise` therefore became a record with an empty
`ExecServerURL` and no connect provider, so environment/info, environment/status,
remote skills and the unified-exec path could not reach the registry executor —
even though `EnvironmentRecord.NoiseProvider` and the Noise dial paths already
existed.

## What changed

- `appserver/environment.go`: `ApplyProviderSnapshot` maps
  `EnvironmentTransportNoise` to `record.NoiseProvider` (and rejects a Noise
  entry without a provider), leaving `ExecServerURL` and `StdioCommand` empty.
