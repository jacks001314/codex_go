## Real gap fixed: #55299 credential renewal

Go had the proxy credential exchange but not Rust #55299's lifecycle: a
credential rejected by an upstream 401 stayed cached, and failed renewals were
retried on every request.

- `app/responses_api_proxy_auth.go`: `responsesProxyAuthLease` carries an
  atomic `rejected` flag; `Lease()` reuses a cached token only while it is not
  rejected, `Invalidate()` kills the current issuance, and failed renewals back
  off 1s, 2s, 4s ... capped at 30s, reporting
  "workload identity renewal temporarily unavailable; retry later" until the
  retry deadline. A successful exchange clears the backoff.
- `app/responses_api_proxy.go`: forwards with `Lease()` when the auth
  implements `responsesProxyAuthLeaseProvider` and invalidates the lease on
  upstream 401 only, without replaying the request; 403 keeps the credential.
- Tests: `TestResponsesProxyFederatedAuthRejectsOldIssuanceOnlyLikeRust`,
  `TestResponsesProxyFederatedAuthBacksOffRenewalFailuresLikeRust`,
  `TestResponsesProxyForwards401WithoutReplayAndRefreshesCredentialLikeRust`,
  `TestResponsesProxyKeepsCredentialOn403LikeRust`, plus the updated
  `TestResponsesProxyFederatedAuthDropsCredentialOnFailedRenewalLikeRust`.
  `go test ./app -run ResponsesProxy -count=1` passes.
