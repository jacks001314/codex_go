  parsed it into `MCPElicitationRequest`, but the user-verification branch of

  `progressToken` was forwarded. The branch now emits `_meta` and
  `parseMCPElicitationRequest` removes `progressToken` for user-verification
  requests, matching Rust `restore_context_meta`.
- #47624 needs no production change: `tool/user_messaging.go` already accepts
  both singular `user_message` connector spellings. Added regression cases for
  the namespaced and flat names plus a negative `send_message_to_thread` case.
- #44288 cannot occur in Go: `os/exec` writes stdin from a copy goroutine while
  concurrently draining stdout/stderr, and the hook deadline is a context
  deadline over the whole command that terminates the process tree. Existing
  `TestHookRunnerFastExitPreservesOutputWithoutReadingLargeStdinLikeRust`
  covers the large-stdin fast-exit path.
- #47292 consolidates Rust's internal `AgentControl` implementation; Go has no
  such trait and already centralizes agent operations on `agent.ToolController`
  and `runtimeAgentController`.
