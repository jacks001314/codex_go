## Final implementation notes

- `tui/session_catalog.go` mirrors Rust `resume_picker/catalog.rs`: cursor,
  mode, pending token, exhaustion, error retention, stale completion rejection,
  and backend stamping are isolated per source.
- `SessionSummary.BackendID` routes a selection to the row's listing source;
  zero-tag rows still inherit the picker owner for backwards compatibility.
- Remote picker pagination now goes through `ThreadCatalog` and preserves the
  existing partial-load notice when a later page fails.
- `ServerRequestResolvedMsg` removes approval, elicitation, and user-input
  views by their JSON-RPC request ID, then settles a paused permission queue.
  Local pickers with a coincident ID are untouched.
- `#57267` cannot be exercised until the core lane supplies a cloud
  conversation transport/front end. The TUI-specific gates are intentionally
  not simulated with dead options.
