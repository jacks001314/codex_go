live tool invocation without a child request, and reserved startup always runs on
the invoking thread's manager.

  `ThreadManager::create_prepared_child` and `prepare_spawn`'s detached path.

  per-thread configuration refresh that no longer carries the requirement keeps
  the parent's exec policy and its source requirements.
