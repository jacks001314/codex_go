## `#40275` Additional generated context fragments

`multi_agent.subagent_notification` content kinds through the typed fragment
path. App-server fragment tests pass.

`daemon_recovery.internal_context`, matching the source-derived kind. App-server
recovery tests pass.

`generic.permissions_instructions` to `permissions.instructions`, with a
regression assertion in the context fragment tests.
