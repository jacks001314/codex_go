## Goal

Bound the Sentry reason tag and align message-board author notification and
subscription semantics.

## Findings

- #47325: `UploadTags` copied the raw report reason into the Sentry tag, which
  may contain newlines or exceed Sentry's 200-character limit.
- #47236: `postInner` already deletes the caller from the merged notification
  recipients, so authors do not receive their own posts even when explicitly
  targeted; subscriptions are untouched.
- #47257: `subscription_opt_outs` already persists explicit unsubscribes,
  `subscribe` honors them, and board deletion clears the table.
- #47259: local `resolvePostDestination` created a channel without subscribing
  its author, while the in-memory board already did. Rust subscribes the author
  in the same transaction that creates the channel.

## Implementation

- `appserver/feedback.go`: add `sentryReasonTag` (first 200 Unicode characters,
  CR/LF to spaces) and use it for the `reason` tag.
- `agentboard/local.go`: pass caller and post behavior into
  `resolvePostDestination` and subscribe the author to a newly created channel
  for discussion posts.
- Tests: `appserver/feedback_test.go`
  `TestUploadReasonTagBoundedLikeRust`; `agentboard/local_test.go`
  `TestLocalBoardNewChannelSubscribesAuthorLikeRust` (explicit self-target,
  creator notification, opt-out preserved).
