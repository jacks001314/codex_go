## Goal

Route opt-in MCP `openai/userVerification` elicitations to one trusted local
connection instead of always cancelling them.

## Findings

- Rust adds typed verification elicitations, routes them to the single
  host-activated connection, rejects responses from any other connection,
  excludes them from replay, cancels on disconnect or authentication changes,
  returns proofs in `content`, and keeps the mode experimental.
- Go already implements the same contract: `mcp.IsUserVerificationElicitation`
  recognizes the method, the app-server handler resolves the owner through
  `RuntimeRouter.userVerificationConnection`, rejects non-owner responses and a
  missing owner with `approvalUnavailable`, maps a closed response channel and
  interruption to the Rust cancellation reasons, and only advertises the
  capability through `SetUserVerificationEnabled`.
- `user_verification_mcp_reason_like_rust_test.go` covers the owner and reason
  mapping; `mcp_elicitation.go` keeps verification out of the ordinary form
  path.

## Implementation
