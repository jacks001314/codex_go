### #38913 Stop rendering columns after filling their area

`tui/src/render/renderable.rs` `ColumnRenderable::render`: adds
`if y >= area.bottom() { break; }` before rendering each child. The
pre-change loop already computed `child_area = Rect(...).intersection(area)`
and only called `child.render(child_area, buf)` when the intersection was
non-empty, so out-of-area children were never painted and their render() was
never invoked; `desired_height` is pure. The change therefore only shortens
the iteration - the rendered output is byte-identical. Go's TUI clips
out-of-area content equivalently (viewport + intersection), so no Go work is
required.
