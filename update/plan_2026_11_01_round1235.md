## #46333 disabled sandbox accounts during cleanup

Most of the commit was already carried: `cleanup_logon_pending` is persisted
before an account is enabled and cleared only after it is disabled again,
`RestoreDisabledRuntimeAccounts` runs before owner restoration and IPC
admission, `CanResumeRegistration`/`AdmitOwner`/`ReadyForPackage` block
readiness while recovery is pending, and `logCleanupError` suppresses repeated
identical events.

The one gap was the deadline diagnostic. `sandboxLogonSessionRemains` returned a
bool and `stopSandboxProcesses` answered every timeout with
"sandbox processes or logon tokens remain after the uninstall deadline". It now
uses `sandboxLogonBlocker`, which names the session the way Rust's
`blocking_logon_session` does (`sandbox logon %08x:%08x remains (logon type %d,
session %d)`, or `LSA returned no data for logon %08x:%08x`), and reports it
through `cleanupBlockedError`: `sandbox cleanup blocked after the uninstall
deadline: <blocker>`. `uninstall_cleanup_windows_test.go` covers both forms.
