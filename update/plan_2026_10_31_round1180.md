## Evidence

- `chatgptapi/backend_models.go`: `AdditionalRateLimitDetails` decodes the
  usage endpoint's extra `normal_model_slug` (Rust keeps it outside the backend
  OpenAPI model because only this endpoint sends it) and
  `RateLimitSnapshot` carries `NormalModelSlug`; the additional snapshot is
  copied with the slug, the primary `codex` snapshot keeps nil, matching
  `make_additional_rate_limit_snapshot` plus
  `snapshot.normal_model_slug = limit.normal_model_slug` in
  `backend-client/src/client/rate_limit_resets.rs`.
- `appserver/runtime_router.go` `accountRateLimitFromBackend` copies the slug
  into `auth.RateLimitSnapshot`, which serializes as `normalModelSlug`.
- `model/usage_limit.go` already keeps Rust's `protocol/src/error.rs` rule that
  a `gpt-reserve` limit name does not suggest switching models.
- Daemon parity was verified rather than changed: `StartWithFeatures` merges
  saved overrides, `RestartWithFeatures` requires a running managed backend and
  skips the restart when the merged overrides match,
  `LifecycleRunner.restart` persists settings only after the old process stops,
  and `app/daemon_recovery.go` plus `tui/onboarding/daemon_recovery.go` port the
  recovery screen (cancel preselected, managed-only restart, one re-check) with
  `app/daemon_startup.go`'s `--no-daemon` guidance for noninteractive required
  failures.
