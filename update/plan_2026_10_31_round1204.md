# Round 1204: audio file references and explicit rejection (2026-10-31)

Target: Rust main at 67f0994b532b8740f14b5b738af7b7b2a915b1d6 (#58430,

## 58430

Rust lets audio inputs carry an uploaded file reference:

- App-server `UserInput::Audio` flattens an untagged `AudioReference`:
  `{"type":"audio","url":...}` stays inline and `{"type":"audio","fileId":...}`
  references an upload; unknown extra fields are dropped.
- Model content `ContentItem::InputAudio` flattens the same reference with
  snake-case field names (`audio_url` / `file_id`).
- History reconstruction and migration interleave inline and file-backed audio
  in their original order; file durations are unavailable, so estimation charges
  `FILE_AUDIO_TOKENS_ESTIMATE` (20 minutes at 10 tokens/second).
- Uploaded audio stays disabled: turn start and steering fail with
  `UnsupportedOperation("Uploaded audio file inputs are not enabled in this build.")`
  after recording the accompanying text, and raw history injection fails with
  `InvalidRequest("Uploaded audio files must be submitted as turn input.")`.

Go mirrors the parts that have carriers:

- `turn.TurnUserInput` already accepts `fileId` next to `url`, so both wire
  shapes decode. `appserver/audio_reference.go` ports Rust's two rejection
  messages and validators: `validateTurnUserInputAudioFiles` runs from the
  turn/start and turn/steer admission paths in `appserver/runtime_router.go`,
  and `validateResponseItemAudioFiles` runs from `thread/inject_items` in both
  `appserver/router.go` and the runtime dispatcher. The untagged wire shape
  prefers the inline arm, so a non-empty `url` wins over a stray `fileId`.
- `appserver/protocol.go` keeps `file_id`/`fileId` on
  `session.ContentPart.FileID` for audio blocks, and the session-to-thread,
  session-to-compact and compact-to-session conversions preserve it, so mixed
  inline and file-backed audio keep their content positions.
- `compact.FileAudioTokensEstimate` (12000) and `compactAudioReferenceBytes`
  mirror `FILE_AUDIO_TOKENS_ESTIMATE` and `estimate_audio_reference_bytes`:
  file-backed audio without a local duration is budgeted at the Responses
  20-minute maximum, while inline audio still uses the decoded duration.

Residual (recorded rather than invented):

- Rust rejects uploaded audio inside the turn runner: `run_turn` and
  `run_hooks_and_record_inputs` record the filtered input (text preserved) and
  fail the turn, and `tasks/mod.rs` reports the error on completion. Those
  carriers are `appserver/turn_runtime.go` and the sampling loop, which a
  parallel agent owns. Go therefore rejects at admission (JSON-RPC invalid
  request) instead of a completed turn carrying an error, and the text is not
  retained for a rejected turn.
- The live turn-input parse that would emit a model `input_audio { file_id }`
  block sits in `appserver/turn_runtime.go`; because uploaded audio must stay
  rejected, not forwarding it is the intended runtime behavior today.

Coverage: `appserver/audio_reference_like_rust_test.go` (wire shapes, inline
precedence, both rejection messages, raw-inject rejection without history
mutation, mixed audio ordering through history reconstruction) and
`compact/audio_file_budget_like_rust_test.go` (12000-token file budget and
mixed audio accounting).
