## 58434

Rust maps the provider audio validation codes (audio_too_large,
audio_too_short, invalid_audio, invalid_audio_data, invalid_audio_format,
missing_audio_sample_rate) to CodexErrorDetails::InvalidAudioInput for
statusless errors and HTTP 400, keeps the provider message with the
"Invalid audio input." fallback, and reports BadRequest without retries.

Go mirrors it through:

- codexapi.ErrorInvalidAudioInput with message-preserving display.
- model.terminalProviderError (HTTP and streamed errors),
  responseFailedError (response.failed), and websocketTerminalProviderError
  (WebSocket error events) classify the six codes; a non-400 HTTP status or a
  WebSocket status outside 0/400 keeps the previous classification.
- isRetryableResponsesStreamError lists the kind as terminal; the guardian
  review retry policy already fails closed for unknown kinds.
- codexapi.ResponsesWebsocketProbeError classifies a rejected upgrade body so
  doctor renders "handshake error: invalid audio input: [redacted]" without
  echoing the body.
- codexapi.ResponseAPIInvalidAudioInput groups the kind under the
  response-debug "invalid request" telemetry label.
