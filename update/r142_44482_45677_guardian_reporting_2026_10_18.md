# #44482 and #45677 Guardian retry reporting and interruption

## Upstream changes

- `#44482` reports exhausted retries and non-transient review failures as a
  review failure rather than a safety determination, keeps the action denied,
  and honors a server-provided retry delay.
- `#45677` moves review reporting and denial accounting into the Guardian
  extension so an extension-requested interruption cancels the active turn and
  emits the interrupted/idle lifecycle.

## Go status

Complete by equivalent implementation.

`guardian_retry_failure_like_rust_test.go` verifies that a non-transient error
is not retried, leaves risk and authorization unset, uses the review-failure
message, and denies the action; it also verifies that a retryable rate limit
honors the server delay.

`guardian_extension_interruption_like_rust_test.go` verifies that the
circuit-breaker interruption cancels the active turn and emits the idle thread
status.
