## Findings

  #41916/#41918 are not applicable).

  asynchronous flush share a clock under paused tests. Go already uses the
  injectable `Model.currentTime` for `noteComposerRunes`, the paste-enter
  window, and Tab/Enter burst checks; `TestComposerPasteBurstKeepsMultilinePaste`
  advances that clock across the burst and its expiry.

  child reloads through the parent's live control. Go has no AgentControl
  trait; `RuntimeRouter.prepareParentOwnedV2ChildResume` already reloads a
  spawn-recorded V2 child only while its parent is loaded, keeps the stored
  child model, and rejects public resume overrides.
