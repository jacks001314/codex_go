## Findings

- Rust #43090 connects captured microphone audio to the outgoing WebRTC track:
  resampling to 48 kHz, echo cancellation/noise suppression/gain control,
  20 ms mono Opus packets, RTP timestamps that advance across mute gaps without
  sequence gaps, mute transitions that clear pending audio and processing
  history, and bounded capture/render queues with a 500 ms processing deadline
  and a 100 ms send timeout.
- The Go helper already implements these behaviors: `pcmPipeline` holds the
  bounded queues and mute generations, `mediaSession.pumpCapture` runs the
  audio processor and packs 20 ms frames, `voiceTrack` owns the Opus RTP clock,
  and `mediaSession.setControls` drops pending capture and resets processing
  history on mute (`voiceSendTimeout` 100 ms, `audioProcessingDeadline` 500 ms,
  `audioCaptureGapTolerance` 20 ms, 64-packet inbound pass).
- Device-rate conversion differs by design: the Go helper always opens devices
  as 48 kHz mono S16LE and lets the miniaudio backend convert, while
  `dsp_resample` covers non-48 kHz formats; Rust resamples in-process with
  Rubato.
