## Findings

- #47623 exposed a real ordering gap in Go's bwrap builder: writable roots were
  bound in policy order, and every protected read-only carveout was mounted in a
  single pass after all binds. A parent carveout that contains a nested writable
  root therefore remounted read-only over that root, and a nested root listed
  before its parent was shadowed by the parent bind.
- Go now mirrors Rust's `sorted_writable_roots`: writable roots bind
  shallowest-first, and each root's carveouts are mounted immediately after its
  own bind, attributed to the deepest writable root containing them. A parent's
  read-only view therefore lands before the deeper writable bind, and carveouts
  owned by the deeper root are mounted exactly once, after that bind.
- Rust's deferral bookkeeping (`deferred_read_only_subpaths`) has no direct Go
  counterpart because Go never emits a per-parent copy in the first place; the
  observable end state (one post-bind mount per shared metadata path) matches.
