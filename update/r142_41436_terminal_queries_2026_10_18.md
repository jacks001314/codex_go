# Rust #41436: respond to terminal queries from TTY subprocesses

Upstream commit: `0ae94fdd49b05ee7faa4d984d06a68492cb32b54`

## Result

Equivalent implementation already present.

- `tool/terminal_queries.go` and `execserver/terminal_queries.go` answer
  device-status (`ESC[5n`), window-size (`ESC[18t`), cursor-position
  (`ESC[6n`), and bounded DEC private-mode queries.
- Handled queries are removed from the output stream; other escape sequences
  and normal output pass through, and queries split across chunks remain
  pending until complete.
- Both unified-exec TTY (`tool/unified_exec.go`) and exec-server TTY
  (`execserver/server.go`) wrap their output readers with the responder.
