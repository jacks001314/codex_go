# Round 1226: Managed-account removal preparation and flag recovery (2026-11-01)

Target: Rust main at 67f0994b532b8740f14b5b738af7b7b2a915b1d6 (#45550,
`registered_runtime/removal.rs` and its embedded `removal.ps1`; #42375).

## Context

Removing a registered runtime must log on the exact recorded accounts first,
because the native cleanup child needs their tokens to delete their packages
and profiles, and it may only proceed after a SYSTEM-only guard owns those
profiles. A service restart between temporarily enabling an account and
re-disabling it must not lose the obligation.

## Preparation

`windowsandboxservice/registered_removal_windows.go` ports
`registered_runtime::removal`:

- `PrepareRuntimeRemoval` refuses an already-retiring runtime, re-queries each
  account, refuses a removed account whose package is still registered, and
  persists `cleanup_logon_pending` plus a revoked ready receipt before it
  temporarily enables a disabled account.
- Logons run under the retained owner token through the pinned secrets
  directory, the account SID is re-validated before the account is re-disabled,
  and the logon target is validated (changed SID, replacement account,
  administrator, sandbox-group membership).
- The retirement generation, revoked receipt, plan (both installation keys,
  record, per-account tokens, service name, sandbox group SID) and the
  `READY` handshake follow Rust: PowerShell is resolved from the system
  directory and must be a regular non-reparse image, tokens are duplicated into
  the exact child with inheritance disabled, and the parent waits for
  `READY\r\n` within 30 s.
- `Commit` writes `COMMIT\n` and keeps the only pipe writer open until this
  service exits; an uncommitted preparation aborts by closing stdin, which is
  the EOF fence.

`RestoreDisabledRuntimeAccounts` ports `restore_disabled_accounts`: under the
setup lock, for the current package family only, every account still marked
`cleanup_logon_pending` is re-validated by SID, re-disabled and cleared, so a
service restart cannot leave a temporary enable behind.

The embedded `assets/removal.ps1` is the Rust asset verbatim.
